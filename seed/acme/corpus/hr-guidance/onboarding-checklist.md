# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

**Owner:** Alice Moreau, HR Manager
**Applies to:** All new hires at Acme Robotics (Rotterdam HQ and remote)
**Last reviewed:** 2026-11-02

This checklist covers every new hire from signed offer to the end of their first month. HR keeps a copy in the employee's onboarding file and ticks off each item as it is done. If a step is blocked, flag it to Alice Moreau the same day.

## 1. After the Offer Is Signed

- [ ] Confirm the start date, role, department and hiring manager in the HR system
- [ ] Send the welcome email with first-day logistics (arrival time, address, who to ask for)
- [ ] Collect signed contract, ID copy, bank details and tax forms
- [ ] Ask the hiring manager to nominate an **onboarding buddy from the new hire's own department**
- [ ] Book the 6-week probation check-in in the calendars of the new hire, the manager and HR
- [ ] Notify Erin Takahashi (IT Administrator) of the start date, role and any special hardware or software needs

## 2. Before the Start Date

### IT Setup

IT (Erin Takahashi) prepares the laptop and all accounts **at least 3 working days before the start date**. This gives time to fix problems before day one.

| Item | Responsible | Done |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop imaged and labelled | Erin Takahashi | ☐ |
| Email, Slack and SSO accounts created | Erin Takahashi | ☐ |
| FleetOS staging access (engineering, support, sales engineering only) | Erin Takahashi | ☐ |
| Building access badge requested | Olivia Grant's Operations team | ☐ |
| Desk assigned | Operations | ☐ |

### Buddy and Manager Prep

- [ ] Buddy confirmed and briefed on their responsibilities
- [ ] Manager prepares a first-week plan with 2–3 concrete starter tasks
- [ ] Team informed of the new colleague's name and start date

## 3. First Day

- [ ] HR welcome and paperwork walkthrough
- [ ] Laptop handover and login check with IT
- [ ] Office tour, including the robot test floor safety briefing (mandatory before entering the test area)
- [ ] Introduction to the onboarding buddy
- [ ] Lunch with the team and buddy
- [ ] Manager 1:1 to go through the first-week plan

## 4. First Week

- [ ] Company overview session: our history since 2019, the AMR-200 and AMR-500 robots, and the FleetOS platform
- [ ] Complete mandatory training: information security, GDPR basics, workplace health and safety
- [ ] Shadow a colleague in their department for at least one session
- [ ] Department-specific introductions (e.g. Support with Samuel Mensah, Sales with Diego Alvarez, engineering with the relevant tech lead)
- [ ] Buddy check-in at the end of the week

## 5. First Month

The onboarding buddy supports the new hire for the **first 30 days**. They answer day-to-day questions, explain how things really work and help the new hire build their network.

- [ ] Weekly buddy check-ins (informal, 20–30 minutes)
- [ ] Weekly manager 1:1s
- [ ] Goals for the probation period agreed in writing with the manager
- [ ] HR pulse survey sent at the end of week 4
- [ ] Buddy arrangement formally closed at day 30; buddy gives brief feedback to HR

## 6. Probation

The probation period is **3 months**. A structured check-in takes place at **6 weeks**.

| Milestone | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 6-week check-in | New hire, manager, HR | Review progress against goals, give two-way feedback, flag any concerns early |
| End of 3 months | Manager, HR | Confirm the end of probation or agree next steps |

HR records the outcome of both meetings in the employee file.
