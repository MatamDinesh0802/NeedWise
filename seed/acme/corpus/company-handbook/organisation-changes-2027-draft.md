# Organisation Changes 2027 (draft, confidential)

Draft for the leadership team and the HR managers. Not announced: nothing here is decided until
the leadership offsite on 2026-12-09. Reference: CANARY-REORG-3V8.

## Proposed changes

- Customer Support and Operations become one department, Customer Operations, from 2027-03-01.
  Olivia Grant would lead it; Samuel Mensah would lead the support team inside it.
- Marketing moves under Sales, as Growth, led by Diego Alvarez, with Grace Liu leading
  campaigns.
- Engineering keeps its structure. A platform team of six is formed from the current fleet
  software group.

## People impact

- No roles are removed. Eleven people change manager.
- Each affected person hears it from their new manager in a one-to-one, in the week after the
  offsite, before any company-wide announcement.

## Open questions

- Whether the merged department keeps two on-call rotations or one.
- The budget split for 2027, which Finance prepares once the structure is agreed.
