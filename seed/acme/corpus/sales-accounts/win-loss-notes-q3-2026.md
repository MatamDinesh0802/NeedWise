## Wins

### Nordlicht Grocers: 25 AMR-200 robots

We won Nordlicht Grocers with an order of **25 AMR-200 robots** and a FleetOS subscription. The robots will run in their chilled distribution centre.

**What worked:**
- **Footprint.** Their aisles are narrow, and the AMR-200 fit them without any racking changes. The site walk-through convinced their operations director more than the slide deck did.
- **FleetOS dashboards.** Their team liked being able to see utilisation and charging status per shift. Their main stakeholder asked for a FleetOS login during the trial so he could check the numbers himself.
- **Pilot structure.** A short, clearly scoped pilot with agreed success metrics shortened the decision cycle. We should keep using this pattern.
- **Support confidence.** Samuel Mensah joined one call to walk through escalation paths and response times. The customer named this as a deciding factor.

**Watch-outs for delivery:**
- Cold-environment battery performance came up several times. Olivia Grant's team should confirm charging schedules before go-live.
- The customer expects to expand later. The account owner should plan a check-in after the first operating quarter.

## Losses

### Harbor Logistics: lost on price

We **lost the Harbor Logistics deal to a competitor on price**. Our technical evaluation went well, and their engineering lead said privately that the AMR-500 performed better on payload handling during the demo. Procurement made the final call, and our proposal came in higher on total upfront cost.

**What we heard:**
- The competitor offered lower hardware pricing and bundled software for the first contract year.
- We could not show the long-term cost advantage of FleetOS early enough. By the time we presented a total-cost-of-ownership comparison, procurement had already shortlisted on headline price.
- Our discount approval took longer than the customer's decision window.

**Lessons:**
1. Bring procurement in earlier and lead with a TCO model, not hardware price alone.
2. Agree discount guardrails with Finance before a proposal goes out, so we can respond within days, not weeks.
3. Keep the relationship warm. The engineering lead was positive about us, and their competitor contract may come up for review.

## Cross-deal themes

- **Price sensitivity is rising** in third-party logistics. Grocery and retail buyers weigh fit and reliability more heavily.
- **FleetOS is a differentiator when buyers see it live.** Demos that show the platform early perform better than spec sheets.
- **Speed of commercial response matters.** Slow internal approvals cost us at least one deal this quarter.
