# IP and Invention Assignment Policy

**Document owner:** Legal & Compliance (legal@acme.test)
**Approved by:** Marcus Bell, CTO, and Priya Raman, CEO
**Effective:** 2026-01-15

## 1. Who owns what you create

Inventions, software, designs, documentation and data that employees create in the course of their work belong to Acme Robotics. This includes work on the AMR-200, the AMR-500, FleetOS and our internal tools, whether it was done in the office, at a customer site or at home. Employment contracts confirm this assignment; contractors assign their work to Acme in the contractor agreement, which HR (Alice Moreau) manages.

## 2. Reporting an invention

If you think you have made something new and useful (a navigation method, a charging technique, a FleetOS scheduling idea), submit the Invention Disclosure Form to legal@acme.test within **30 days** of the idea first working. Do not publish, present or demo it outside Acme before Legal & Compliance has assessed it: publication can make a patent impossible.

Legal & Compliance decides within 60 days whether Acme files a patent application, keeps the invention as a trade secret, or releases it.

## 3. Inventor awards

Every named inventor on an Acme patent application receives:

| Milestone | Award per inventor |
|---|---|
| Patent application filed | 1,000 euros |
| Patent granted | 2,500 euros |

Awards are paid with the next monthly salary. Acme currently holds 6 granted patents, with 4 applications pending.

## 4. Open source

- You may contribute to the open-source projects on the approved list kept by the CTO, Marcus Bell. Contributions to any other project need his written approval.
- FleetOS source code is never published.
- Code under copyleft licences (GPL, AGPL) may not be added to FleetOS or robot firmware without approval from the CTO and Legal & Compliance.

## 5. Side projects

Side projects in your own time, on your own equipment and unrelated to Acme's business remain yours. Tell legal@acme.test about a side project that touches robotics, warehouse software or logistics before you start, so we can confirm in writing that Acme claims no rights in it.

## 6. Questions

Ask legal@acme.test, or Marcus Bell for questions about open-source contributions.
