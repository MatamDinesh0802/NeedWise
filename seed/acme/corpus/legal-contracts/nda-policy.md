# NDA Policy

**Document owner:** Legal & Compliance (legal@acme.test)
**Approved by:** Priya Raman, CEO
**Effective:** 2026-03-02
**Applies to:** Everyone who shares or receives confidential information with another company on Acme Robotics' behalf

## 1. Why we use NDAs

Prospects, suppliers and partners often need to see roadmaps, pricing, site layouts or technical details before a deal exists. A non-disclosure agreement (NDA) protects that information both ways. Most NDAs are routine, so we keep them fast: the standard mutual NDA is self-service.

## 2. When you may use the standard mutual NDA without legal review

You may sign the standard mutual NDA template **LEG-NDA-02** (version 2026-03) yourself, without sending it to Legal & Compliance, when **all** of these are true:

1. The other party is a prospective customer, supplier or partner evaluating a business relationship with Acme.
2. You change nothing in the template except the names, addresses and the description of the purpose.
3. The agreement term is at most 3 years. Confidentiality obligations then survive for 5 years after the term ends; do not change this.
4. No source code, unreleased firmware or personal data will be shared under it.
5. The other party is not a competitor of Acme.

The template is governed by Dutch law, with the courts of Rotterdam as the forum. Any department manager may sign it in the company e-signature tool (see the Contract Signature Authority Matrix).

## 3. When Legal & Compliance must review

Send the NDA to legal@acme.test before anyone signs it when:

- the other party wants to use its own NDA, or edits ours in any way;
- the NDA is one-way (only one party discloses);
- source code, unreleased firmware or personal data will be shared;
- the other party is a competitor (these also need the CEO's approval);
- the term would exceed 3 years.

Legal & Compliance answers NDA reviews within **3 business days**. A reviewed NDA is signed by the CFO (Hannah Schmidt).

## 4. After signing

- Upload the signed NDA to ContractHub within 5 business days of signature, tagged with the counterparty and expiry date.
- Share only what the stated purpose needs, and mark documents "Confidential".
- When the relationship ends, ask the other party to return or destroy our information.

## 5. Employees and contractors

This policy covers NDAs with other companies. Confidentiality for employees is part of their employment contract. Contractors sign the contractor NDA before they receive repository access; HR (Alice Moreau) sends it and keeps it on file.

## 6. Questions

Ask legal@acme.test. If unsure whether a case qualifies for the standard NDA, ask before you send anything confidential.
