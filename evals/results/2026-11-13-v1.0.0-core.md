### qa-12 (dan): Do I have to declare gifts from suppliers?
- unsupported claim: A gift from a supplier worth up to €50 does not have to be declared.

> It depends on the value of the gift and what it is:
> 
> - **Up to €50:** You don't have to declare it. You may accept it if it is occasional and not linked to a pending decision [S1, S2].
> - **Above €50:** Yes, declare it to Finance (Hannah Schmidt's team) in the gift register, either before or immediately after accepting [S1, S2].
> - **Cash, vouchers or gift cards:** Never accept them, whatever the value [S1, S2].
> - **Hospitality during an active tender or negotiation:** Decline it and inform your manager [S1, S2].
> 
> When in doubt, declare. A declaration is never a problem, but an undisclosed gift can be [S2].
> 
> If you are involved in selecting a supplier, the contract review checklist also requires confirming that no gifts or hospitality were accepted from that supplier during the selection [S4].

### pp-04 (bob): Can I spend a month working from my parents' place in Spain?
- unsupported claim: The rest of the month could be taken as leave.

> Probably not as a full month. Working from another country is limited to 20 days per year and requires HR approval [S1]. A month would exceed that limit.
> 
> If you want to work from Spain for part of that time:
> - Submit a request to HR before you travel, stating the destination, the dates and the work you plan to do [S1].
> - Approval depends on tax, social security, data protection and immigration considerations [S1].
> - Do not start working from abroad until HR has confirmed approval in writing [S1].
> - Access to customer data and FleetOS production systems from abroad must follow IT security guidance [S1].
> 
> The rest of the month could be taken as leave, which is requested through the HR portal. Requests for absences longer than 3 days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance [S2].

### pp-14 (bob): How do customer integrations prove who they are when calling our robot API?
- unsupported claim: The expires_in value of 3600 is in seconds.

> Customer integrations authenticate with OAuth 2.0 client credentials, issued per customer. Your Acme Robotics onboarding contact gives your organisation a `client_id` and `client_secret`, and a token can only reach robots in your own fleet [S1].
> 
> How it works:
> - The client sends a `POST /oauth/token` request with `grant_type=client_credentials`, its `client_id` and its `client_secret` [S2].
> - The response contains an `access_token` of type Bearer, with `expires_in` set to 3600 (seconds) in the example [S2].
> - The client then sends the token in the `Authorization: Bearer <token>` header on every request [S2].
> 
> Keep the client secret on the server side, and never embed it in browser or handheld-scanner applications [S2]. To rotate credentials, open a request with Acme Robotics Support [S2].
