## Notes

- Baseline before tuning: main stack (Enterprise dev license, real embeddings from the host's llama.cpp server, ASSISTANT_MODE=auto), every model call to the stub (loadtests/compose.stub.yaml), Postgres with pg_stat_statements.
- The one failure (documents: open, 404) was a reader opening a document the uploader had just deleted: a correct answer. The harness now keeps readers out of the upload collection.
