## Notes

- Stress step beyond the realistic range, baseline before tuning: same stack and stub; Locust in 4 processes.
- The 15 failed conversation deletes came from Locust worker processes of the 200-user run before this one: they outlived it, then stopped their users with that run's revoked tokens and reported to this run's master. The harness now stops Locust's process group after each run. No request of this run's users failed.
